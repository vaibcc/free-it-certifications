Know before you enrol

How the labels work

We separate learning access from the cost of earning a formal credential.

Free learning + free credential

The course and its stated completion credential are available without payment. Optional upgrades may still exist.

Free learning + paid credential

You can study for free, but an exam, proctored assessment or formal certification usually costs extra.

Conditional or limited offer

Free access depends on eligibility, a promotion, a voucher, a subscription tier or regional availability.
